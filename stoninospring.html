About the Place

Sto. Niño Cold Spring is one of Camiguin’s most popular natural attractions, known for its crystal-clear and refreshingly cool waters. Surrounded by lush greenery and tall trees, it provides a relaxing escape from the tropical heat.

The spring features a large swimming pool fed by natural spring water, making it perfect for families and tourists looking to unwind. Its cool temperature stays consistent year-round, offering a unique and refreshing experience.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Catarman, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 💧 Type: Natural cold spring pool
  • 🌡️ Water Temperature: Cool (around 20°C)
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning or midday
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring extra clothes and waterproof bag
  • 📸 Famous for: Clear blue waters & relaxing forest setting
