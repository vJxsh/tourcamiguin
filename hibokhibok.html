About the Place

Mount Hibok-Hibok is an active stratovolcano located in Camiguin, near the town of Mambajao. It is the island’s most famous peak and offers a challenging yet rewarding hiking experience for adventure seekers.

The mountain is known for its rich biodiversity, hot and cold springs, and panoramic summit views overlooking the island and the Bohol Sea. Its historical eruptions have shaped much of Camiguin’s landscape, making it both a natural wonder and an important geological site.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Mambajao, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 🌋 Type: Active stratovolcano
  • 🥾 Activity: Hiking & trekking
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Early morning (start of hike)
  • 🎒 Tip: Hire a local guide and bring enough water
  • 📸 Famous for: Summit views & volcanic landscapes
