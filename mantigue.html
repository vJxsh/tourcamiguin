About the Place

Mantigue Island is a small, uninhabited island off the coast of Mahinog in Camiguin, known for its powdery white sand beaches and crystal-clear waters. Surrounded by a vibrant marine sanctuary, it is one of the island province's top destinations for relaxation and nature lovers.

Visitors can enjoy swimming, snorkeling, and beach picnics while taking in the stunning views of Mount Hibok-Hibok in the distance. The island's peaceful atmosphere and well-preserved environment make it a perfect tropical getaway.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Mahinog, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 🏝️ Type: White sand island & marine sanctuary
  • 🏊 Activity: Swimming, snorkeling, beach picnics
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning (calmer seas)
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring snorkeling gear and food supplies
  • 📸 Famous for: Clear waters, white sand, and coral reefs
