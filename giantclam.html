About the Place

The Giant Clam Sanctuary in Camiguin is a protected marine area dedicated to the conservation and breeding of giant clams, locally known as “taklobo.” It is located in the coastal waters of Guinsiliban and plays an important role in preserving marine biodiversity.

Visitors can snorkel in the clear waters to see various species of giant clams in different sizes and vibrant colors. The sanctuary offers an educational and eco-friendly experience, showcasing the importance of marine conservation.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Guinsiliban, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 🐚 Type: Marine sanctuary
  • 🤿 Activity: Snorkeling & marine viewing
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning (clearer waters)
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring snorkeling gear and reef-safe sunscreen
  • 📸 Famous for: Colorful giant clams & conservation efforts
