About the Place

Ardent Hot Springs is one of Camiguin's most popular natural attractions, located at the foot of Mount Hibok-Hibok in Mambajao. The springs are naturally heated by volcanic activity, providing warm, soothing waters perfect for relaxation.

The area features multiple cascading pools surrounded by lush forest, creating a peaceful and refreshing environment. Whether you visit during the day or at night, Ardent Hot Springs offers a relaxing experience for travelers looking to unwind.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Mambajao, Camiguin, Philippines
  • ♨️ Type: Natural hot spring resort
  • 🏊 Activity: Soaking & relaxation
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Evening or nighttime
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring swimwear and extra clothes
  • 📸 Famous for: Warm cascading pools & forest setting
