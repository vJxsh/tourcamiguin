About the Place

The Guiob Church Ruins, also known as the Old Catarman Church Ruins, are one of Camiguin’s most important historical landmarks. Built during the Spanish colonial period, the church was destroyed by the eruption of Mount Vulcan in the 1870s.

Today, the remaining coral stone walls stand as a reminder of the island’s dramatic volcanic history. Visitors can explore the ruins and walk through the preserved structures, offering a glimpse into the past and a peaceful, reflective atmosphere.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Catarman, Camiguin, Philippines
  • ⛪ Type: Historical church ruins
  • 📜 Built: Spanish colonial era
  • 🌋 Destroyed: 1870s volcanic eruption
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning or late afternoon
  • 📸 Famous for: Coral stone ruins & historical significance
