About Lanzones

Lanzones, known locally as “Lansones,” is a small tropical fruit that has become Camiguin's most celebrated export. The island's volcanic soil and tropical climate make it ideal for growing sweet, juicy, and flavorful lanzones.

The fruit is harvested from August to October and is a major part of the province's culture. Camiguin even celebrates the annual Lanzones Festival, a colorful event featuring street dancing, parades, and cultural presentations, honoring the fruit and local farmers who make it possible.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Mambajao & surrounding towns, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 🍇 Type: Tropical fruit
  • 🛒 Activity: Harvesting & local markets
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: August - October (harvest season)
  • 🎒 Tip: Try fresh lanzones and local products like jams
  • 📸 Famous for: Sweetness, juicy flavor, and the Lanzones Festival
  • 🌱 Fun Fact: Volcanic soil gives the fruit its distinct sweetness
