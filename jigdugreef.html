About Jigdug Reef

Jigdug Reef is one of Camiguin's most beautiful marine sites, located off the coast near the town of Mambajao. Known for its crystal-clear waters and thriving coral gardens, it is a haven for snorkelers and divers looking to explore vibrant underwater life.

The reef is home to a variety of tropical fish, colorful corals, and other marine species, making it an important site for marine biodiversity. Guided snorkeling tours are available, giving visitors a safe and educational way to experience the reef's natural beauty.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Near Mambajao, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 🐠 Type: Coral reef / marine biodiversity site
  • 🤿 Activity: Snorkeling & diving
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning for calmer waters
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring snorkeling gear and reef-safe sunscreen
  • 📸 Famous for: Colorful corals, tropical fish, and clear waters
  • 🌊 Fun Fact: Jigdug Reef is ideal for beginners and families due to its shallow areas
