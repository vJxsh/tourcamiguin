About the Place

Macau Cold Spring is a peaceful and refreshing natural attraction located in the town of Catarman, Camiguin. Known for its cool, crystal-clear waters, the spring provides a relaxing escape from the island's tropical heat.

Surrounded by lush greenery, the spring features a natural pool perfect for swimming and unwinding. Its quiet atmosphere makes it a great alternative to more crowded tourist spots, offering a calm and refreshing experience for visitors.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Catarman, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 💧 Type: Natural cold spring
  • 🏊 Activity: Swimming & relaxation
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning or midday
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring snacks and extra clothes
  • 📸 Famous for: Cool clear waters & peaceful surroundings
