About the Festival

The Lanzones Festival is an annual celebration held in Camiguin every October to honor the province's sweetest export: the lanzones fruit. The festival showcases the rich agricultural heritage of the island and pays tribute to the farmers who grow the prized tropical fruit.

Visitors can enjoy street dancing, colorful parades, cultural performances, and exhibits featuring local products. The festival is not only a vibrant cultural event but also a chance to taste fresh lanzones and see the community's traditions come alive.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Mambajao & nearby towns, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 🎉 Type: Annual cultural & agricultural festival
  • 🗓️ Month: October (harvest season)
  • 🌿 Activities: Street dancing, parades, exhibits, tasting lanzones
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring your camera and sample local delicacies
  • 📸 Famous for: Colorful costumes, lively dances, and community spirit
  • 🍇 Fun Fact: The festival celebrates the harvest and promotes local agriculture
