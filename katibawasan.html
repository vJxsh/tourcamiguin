About the Place

Katibawasan Falls is one of Camiguin’s most iconic natural attractions, located in the town of Mambajao. The waterfall cascades from a height of approximately 70 meters, creating a refreshing pool at its base surrounded by lush tropical forest.

The cool, misty atmosphere and scenic surroundings make it a perfect spot for relaxation, photography, and nature appreciation. Visitors can enjoy the serene environment and take a dip in the cold, clear waters.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Mambajao, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 💦 Type: Natural waterfall
  • 🏊 Activity: Swimming & sightseeing
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning or after light rain
  • 🎒 Tip: Wear non-slip footwear
  • 📸 Famous for: Tall cascade & lush jungle setting
