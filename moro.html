About the Place

The Moro Watch Tower in Camiguin is a historical structure built during the Spanish colonial period. It served as a lookout point to guard coastal communities against raids by Moro pirates, which were common in the southern Philippines during that time.

Located along the shoreline in Guinsiliban, the tower offers a glimpse into the island’s past and its defensive strategies. Today, it stands as a cultural landmark where visitors can enjoy scenic coastal views while learning about Camiguin’s history.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Guinsiliban, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 🏛️ Type: Historical watch tower
  • 📜 Built: Spanish colonial era
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning or sunset
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring a camera for scenic coastal views
  • 📸 Famous for: Historical significance & oceanfront location
