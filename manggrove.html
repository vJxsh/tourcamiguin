About the Place

Katunggan Mangrove Park is a thriving eco-tourism destination in Camiguin, located in the coastal town of Mahinog. The park is home to a rich mangrove forest that plays a vital role in protecting the shoreline and supporting marine biodiversity.

Visitors can walk along a scenic wooden boardwalk that winds through the mangroves, offering a peaceful nature experience and opportunities to observe wildlife such as crabs, fish, and birds. The park is also a great place to learn about environmental conservation and the importance of mangrove ecosystems.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Mahinog, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 🌿 Type: Mangrove park & eco-tourism site
  • 🚶 Activity: Boardwalk tours, nature viewing
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning or late afternoon
  • 🎒 Tip: Wear comfortable footwear and bring insect repellent
  • 📸 Famous for: Wooden boardwalk & rich mangrove ecosystem
