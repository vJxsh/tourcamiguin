About the Place

White Island is a stunning uninhabited sandbar located just off the northern coast of Camiguin Island in the Philippines. Known for its powdery white sand and crystal-clear waters, it offers breathtaking views of Mount Hibok-Hibok and the surrounding sea.

The island has no trees or shade, making it a perfect spot for sunbathing, photography, and relaxing by the ocean. Visitors usually arrive by small boat from Agoho.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Camiguin, Philippines
  • 🏝️ Type: Sandbar (no permanent structures)
  • ⛵ Travel Time: 10–15 minutes by boat
  • ☀️ Best Time to Visit: Early morning or sunset
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring water, sunscreen, and umbrella
  • 📸 Famous for: 360° ocean views & Mt. Hibok-Hibok backdrop
