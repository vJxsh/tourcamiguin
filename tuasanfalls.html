About the Place

Tuasan Falls is one of Camiguin’s most beautiful and accessible waterfalls, located in the town of Catarman. Surrounded by lush forest and volcanic rock formations, the falls cascade powerfully into a cool, clear basin.

Unlike other remote waterfalls, Tuasan Falls can be easily reached by road, making it a favorite spot for both locals and tourists. Visitors can enjoy swimming in its refreshing waters or simply relax while taking in the scenic natural surroundings.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Catarman, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 💦 Type: Natural waterfall
  • 🚗 Access: Short walk from roadside
  • 🌿 Best Time to Visit: Morning to early afternoon
  • 🎒 Tip: Wear non-slip footwear and bring extra clothes
  • 📸 Famous for: Strong cascade & crystal-clear pool
