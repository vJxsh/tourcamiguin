About the Place

Soda Bora Water Spring is one of Camiguin's most unique natural attractions, known for its naturally carbonated spring water. Located in the town of Catarman, this spring gets its name from the slight “soda-like” fizz that visitors can feel when swimming.

The cool, refreshing waters make it a perfect spot to relax, especially after exploring the island's volcanoes and beaches. Surrounded by lush greenery, Soda Bora offers a peaceful and refreshing escape for both locals and tourists.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Catarman, Camiguin, Philippines
  • 💧 Type: Natural soda water spring
  • 🏊 Activity: Swimming & relaxation
  • 🕒 Best Time to Visit: Morning or late afternoon
  • 🎒 Tip: Bring extra clothes and stay hydrated
  • 📸 Famous for: Naturally carbonated (bubbly) spring water
