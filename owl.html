About the Camiguin Boo Book

The Camiguin boo book, scientifically known as the Camiguin hawk-owl (Ninox leventisi), is a rare owl species found only on the island of Camiguin in the Philippines. Its name “boo book” comes from its distinct hooting call that echoes through the forest at night.

This small owl inhabits forested areas, particularly in the slopes of Mount Hibok-Hibok. It plays an important role in the ecosystem by controlling insect and small animal populations. Because it exists nowhere else in the world, it is considered endemic and highly valuable to biodiversity.

Conservation efforts are important to protect the Camiguin hawk-owl, as habitat loss and environmental changes threaten its population. It is a symbol of the island’s unique wildlife and natural heritage.
